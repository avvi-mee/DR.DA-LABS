Research areas

Five active lines

The through-line is energy: molecules that absorb light and then do something useful with it, whether that is generating current, driving a reaction, or storing charge.

DSSC

Dye-sensitized solar cells

Organic dyes that absorb sunlight and inject electrons into a semiconductor — a cheaper, printable route to photovoltaics than silicon.

Synth

Porphyrin & phthalocyanine synthesis

Building the large ring-shaped macrocycles that serve as both dyes and photocatalysts. The same family of molecule that makes blood red and leaves green.

Photo

Heterogeneous photocatalysis

Solid catalysts that use light to drive chemical reactions, including breaking down pollutants that are otherwise difficult to treat.

Cap

Supercapacitors

Electrode materials for devices that charge and discharge far faster than batteries, characterised by cyclic voltammetry.

Org

Organic transformations

Synthetic methodology — developing cleaner, higher-yielding routes to the intermediates the work above depends on.
