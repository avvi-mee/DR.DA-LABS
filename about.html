About

Dr. Darpan Vijaykumar Bhuse

Assistant Professor of Chemistry at JSPM University, Pune. I spend my working week on solar-cell dyes and my spare hours explaining what is inside a perfume bottle.

The short version

A chemist who got curious about scent

I did not arrive at perfume through fashion. I arrived through synthesis. My doctorate is in the chemistry of porphyrins and phthalocyanines — large, ring-shaped, intensely coloured molecules used to capture light in dye-sensitized solar cells. You spend years learning to read a structure and predict how it will behave, and eventually you start pointing that habit at everything.

Fragrance turned out to be the most interesting thing to point it at. Perfume is one of the few consumer products where the chemistry is the entire experience, and also one where almost nobody is told what they are buying. An ingredient list is treated as a trade secret. A price is justified with a story rather than a material cost. Meanwhile the actual explanations — why a note fades, what a fixative is doing, whether a much-feared ingredient is genuinely a problem at the dose you wear it — are neither difficult nor dangerous to give.

Perfume marketing runs on adjectives. I would rather hand you the structure.

So I started making videos under the name Attar n Atom — attar for the tradition I grew up around, atom for the training. The name is the whole argument: India has one of the oldest continuous perfumery cultures on earth, and it deserves to be explained with the same rigour anyone would apply to a French house.

The channel is still small. I post in English, Hindi and Marathi, because the people I most want to reach do not all read the same one, and because a scientific idea does not become less rigorous for being explained in the language someone actually thinks in.

What I do in the week

I teach chemistry at JSPM University's School of Basic and Applied Sciences, where I have been since August 2023. Before that I was a teaching research assistant at VIT, running laboratory instruction alongside my doctoral work. The teaching came first, and it is still what the videos are — the same job, with a wider room.
