What I cover

Five ways into a bottle

Every subject below comes from a video I have already made. No fragrance-influencer adjectives, no "notes of ambition" — just the chemistry, explained so it lands.

Tox

What is actually in there

Diethyl phthalate sits in a huge share of the perfumes sold in India. What it does to a formulation, why it is there, and what the safety data actually says — separated from the panic.

Olf

How to smell properly

Olfactory fatigue is why your fourth spray in a shop smells like nothing. Where pulse points help and where they are a myth, and how to test a fragrance so your nose is still honest.

Mat

Oud against sandalwood

Two of the most expensive materials in perfumery, constantly confused. Where each one comes from, why the price is what it is, and how to tell them apart on skin.

Hist

Scent has a history

Mysore Sandal Soap exists because of the First World War — Mysore's sandalwood oil had nowhere left to ship. The story of Indian perfumery is an industrial story.

Myth

Facts worth the reel

What a kilo of a raw material really costs. Which perfume molecules keep mosquitoes off you. How "fresh air" is built in a lab, given that air has no smell.

Lab

The day job feeds it

I synthesise porphyrins and phthalocyanines for solar cells. The same analytical habits — read the structure, trust the data — are what I bring to a fragrance.
