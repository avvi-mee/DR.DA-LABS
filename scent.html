Mat · Materials

Oud against sandalwood

Two of the most expensive materials in perfumery, and two of the most confidently confused. Oud is a resin a tree makes when it is infected; sandalwood is heartwood oil that takes decades to be worth distilling. They behave nothing alike on skin, and the price gap has reasons.

Hist · Scent history

Indian perfumery is an industrial story

Mysore Sandal Soap exists because the First World War closed the export routes for Mysore's sandalwood oil, and the state was left holding a stockpile it had to use. Most fragrance traditions have an origin like this — a supply problem, not a romance.
